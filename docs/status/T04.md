## Regular cash-session follow-up

Recorder coverage remains 09:00–15:35 IST; derived regular-session bars use
the half-open interval [09:15, 15:30) IST, with boundaries compared in UTC.
`classify_ticks`, `time_bars`, `volume_bars`, and `candle_bars` now accept explicit
`session_open`/`session_close` `datetime.time` keyword arguments, defaulting to
the runtime clocks in config/default.yaml. Trade audit rows outside the window
are retained with `OUTSIDE_SESSION`. Pre-open, regular, and post-close phases
have separate cumulative-volume baselines: the first usable snapshot in each
phase has zero volume and `FIRST_TICK`; a pre-open baseline cannot enter regular
CVD. Stale and duplicate snapshots do not advance a phase baseline. Quote
history, strictly-earlier quote selection, tick-rule fallback, and snapshot
`Trade.exchange_ts` semantics remain unchanged. Session membership uses the
snapshot exchange timestamp, with receipt time as fallback, rather than `ltt`.

Time and volume bars exclude outside-session snapshots even when their audit
flags were absent. Historical input coverage must lie wholly within the regular
window; overlapping pre-open/post-close candles are excluded. A candle ending
exactly at 15:30 is eligible; a trade snapshot at 15:30 is outside the window.
Incomplete final time buckets remain unclosed and omitted: specifically the
60-minute 15:15–16:15 bucket is never represented as a complete bar, even when
`as_of` is after 16:15. This applies to live and historical resampling. No partial
time-bar row is emitted under the existing schema.
